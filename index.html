🧪 Quick Lab Guide for Students

  1. Select Glassware: Choose a Beaker, Erlenmeyer Flask, Volumetric Flask, or Test Tube.
  2. Add Chemicals: Click or drag Acids, Bases, Salts, Metals, Nonmetals, or Indicators into the vessel.
  3. Adjust Apparatus: Use the Bunsen Burner to heat mixtures, turn on the Magnetic Stirrer, or perform drop-by-drop Titration.
  4. Observe Sensors & Equations: Monitor live pH, temperature, conductivity, precipitates, gas fumes, flames, or UV glow.
  5. Record & Practice: Save your findings to the Lab Notebook or test your knowledge in Quiz Mode.
